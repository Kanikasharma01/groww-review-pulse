# Fee explainer: Brokerage and other charges on stock trades

- Equity brokerage is ₹20 or 0.1% of the order value per executed order, whichever is lower, with a minimum of ₹5.
- Each trade also carries regulatory and statutory charges: STT, stamp duty, exchange transaction charge, SEBI turnover charge and Investor Protection Fund Trust charge. For equity delivery, STT is 0.1% on buy and sell and stamp duty is 0.015% on buy; for equity intraday, STT is 0.025% on sell and stamp duty is 0.003% on buy.
- DP charges apply per sell transaction on equity delivery (depository ₹3.5, or ₹3.25 for women, plus Groww ₹16.5; ₹0 where the debit value is under ₹100). DP charges are ₹0 for intraday.
- An 18% GST applies on brokerage, DP charges, exchange transaction charges, IPFT, SEBI turnover charges, delayed payment charges, DDPI charges, KYC modification charges, API charges and auto square-off charges.
- Some charges apply only in specific cases: auto square-off of open intraday positions is ₹50 per position, delayed payment charges are 0.05% per day, and DDPI activation is ₹100 plus GST.
- Account opening and account maintenance are ₹0. The brokerage calculator estimates the total charges for a trade before it is placed.
